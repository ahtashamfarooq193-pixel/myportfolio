Product overview

SwitchON presents a magnetic interchangeable footwear system built around a simple idea: one shoe base can support multiple looks. Its public launch site introduces interchangeable heels and slippers, style collections and a planned global Kickstarter launch.

The digital experience must explain an unfamiliar product quickly. Visitors need to understand the base-and-skin concept, see the visual variety and move from curiosity toward launch interest without unnecessary friction.

My ongoing contribution

I work on SwitchON as a full-stack developer. The engagement is ongoing, so this case study documents the public product and the type of engineering responsibility involved without publishing private implementation details.

Product-facing interface

Supporting a responsive experience that communicates the interchangeable product concept across desktop and mobile screens.

Application functionality

Contributing across frontend and backend concerns as product requirements and launch functionality continue to evolve.

Content structure

Keeping product categories, visual storytelling and calls to action understandable for first-time visitors.

Iterative delivery

Working within an active product cycle where features and content are refined as the launch progresses.

Current outcome

The current public experience gives SwitchON a focused destination for explaining its magnetic footwear concept and presenting the range of interchangeable styles.
